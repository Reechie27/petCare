PetCare Information Management System

Happy Pets, Healthy Lives & Seamless Clinic Care.

Complete end-to-end management for Pet Owners, Medical Records, Appointments, Vaccinations, Feeding schedules, and Grooming care.

Owner & Pet Registry

Manage registered pet parents and link multiple pets to single owners effortless relational mapping.

Appointment Booking

Schedule consultations with preferred veterinarians, select services, and auto-calculate invoice totals.

Medical & Vaccination

Record clinical diagnoses, prescriptions, treatment histories, and track upcoming booster vaccine deadlines.

Feeding Schedules

Log specialized pet diet brands, daily quantities, and precise feeding times for boarding pets.

Grooming Logs

Keep track of baths, haircuts, nail trimming sessions, and assigned professional groomers.
